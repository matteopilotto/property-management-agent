# Case 016: reasoning

## Facts

- Tenant reported the alarm down on 7 Aug (ev 1).
- Landlord said he would use his own electrician, with no name given (ev 4, 7).
- A fitter attended but could not refit the alarm because the box had no bracket (ev 10).
- The alarm was still unmounted on 11 Sep (ev 13, 14).
- The tenant was hospitalised and asked for reduced contact (ev 11-13).
- An unverified visitor knocked on 20 Sep (ev 15, 16).

## Conflicts

- The tenant said the landlord's work was expected on 31 Aug (ev 9), but later said a fitter, not the landlord, attended (ev 10).
- The landlord's electrician, the fitter and the 20 Sep visitor cannot be matched, because no contractor was ever named.
- Ev 16 says the knock was passed to a building-management contact, but the outcome is unrecorded.

## Missing

- The electrician's identity.
- A confirmed refit date.
- Confirmation that the alarm is mounted and tested.
- The identity and authority of the 20 Sep visitor.

## Options rejected

- Wait: this is a safety item and it has already drifted for weeks.
- Ask only: the safety risk, the unverified visitor and the vulnerable tenant need the property manager's decision.
- Contact the tenant: they asked for a pause in contact (ev 13, 14).
- Close: there is no completion evidence.

## Uncertainty

The landlord may reply and settle the contractor question, but the safety and welfare issues still need the property manager.
