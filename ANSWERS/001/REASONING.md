# Case 001: reasoning

## Facts

- Landlord authorised only an inspection and quote, not the repair (event 5).
- Quotation NBL-001-Q1: £2,370.00 + £474.00 VAT = £2,844.00 (bathroom-works-quotation.pdf, event 16).
- Landlord asked for an itemised breakdown (event 18); the contractor gave materials £1,270, labour £1,170, 10% commission and a 50% deposit (event 21).
- Landlord asked 'Which quote do you mean?' after a vague chase that named no amount (events 22-23).

## Conflicts

- Materials £1,270 + labour £1,170 = £2,440, not the quoted subtotal £2,370 (events 16, 21).
- The quotation PDF names a different property address from the canonical one; treated as noise and not repeated.
- The commission and deposit basis are unclear (event 21).

## Missing

- Corrected, itemised contractor quote for the right property
- Landlord approval of a specific amount
- Deposit basis and commission treatment

## Options rejected

- Escalate: one message to the contractor can settle the figures, so asking comes first.
- Book the contractor: no landlord approval of the quoted work (event 5).
- Wait only: the landlord's question needed an answer.

## Uncertainty

The 'couple of quotes' the landlord mentions may be the original quote and the breakdown in event 21. I don't know if he has seen another document.
