# Case 021: reasoning

## Facts

- Tenant reported no hot water and no boiler power (events 1, 3).
- The landlord is using his own contractor (events 10, 15).
- The tenant said nothing had been done on 11 and 13 May (events 12, 17).
- On 15 May the landlord said his plumber fixed it on 14 May, based on a text, with no report (event 18).
- The tenant has not replied to the 15 May voicemail and email (events 19, 20).
- A letterbox fault was reported on 13 May and is unassessed (event 17).

## Conflicts

- The landlord says it is fixed (event 18). The tenant's last word was that nothing had been done (events 12, 17). The two may simply have crossed in time.

## Missing

- Tenant confirmation that hot water is restored.
- Plumber's report or invoice.
- Letterbox details.

## Options rejected

- Wait: blocked by the gateway as a possible hazard, and the tenant has had no hot water for about 10 days.
- Close: no confirmation from the tenant and no completion evidence.
- Escalate: the landlord has answered each chase, so the case is not yet stuck.
- Instruct an agency contractor: no landlord approval and the landlord says it is fixed.

## Uncertainty

It is not known whether hot water is back. If the tenant says it is not, or stays silent, escalate or push for an agency contractor with landlord approval.
