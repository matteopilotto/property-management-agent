# Case 014: reasoning

## Facts

- Heating failed and the living room was 10°C at 16:25 (events 1, 18). The tenant has a mobility impairment (event 3).
- The £250 authority is per urgent incident, inclusive of call-out, labour and parts (event 7).
- The landlord confirmed the £250 authority stands and the £168 diagnostic can be authorised without further consent (event 14).
- The contractor offered a 15 Jan 09:00-11:00 slot at £168 incl. VAT, bookable only by 17:30. No parts or repair work are included (event 19).
- The tenant can give access on 15 Jan with 30 minutes' notice and a direct call. The agency holds no key (event 13).
- The earlier standard slot of 17 Jan clashes with the tenant's hospital appointment (events 6, 13).
- The duty file records no spending limit for temporary equipment, transport or accommodation (events 7, 17).

## Conflicts

- The landlord asked for a quote before non-emergency spend (event 10) but then clarified that the urgent authority stands (event 14). This is not a real conflict.
- Event 19 says the tenant's direct access confirmation is still needed, though she already offered 15 Jan access in event 13. I have asked her to reconfirm.

## Missing

- The tenant's reconfirmation of access and her contact number for the engineer.
- Landlord or duty-manager decision on temporary heating, transport or accommodation.
- A quote for any repair. Only £82 of the incident limit remains after the diagnostic.

## Options rejected

- Waiting for the landlord: the slot expired at 17:30 and the landlord had already confirmed the authority.
- Keeping the 17 Jan standard slot: it is too late for a cold home and the tenant cannot attend.
- Committing spend on heaters, transport or accommodation: there is no authority for it.
- Escalating: the booking is within authority. The landlord was told the interim items need his decision, and the duty manager is already involved (event 17).

## Uncertainty

The tenant has not yet reconfirmed access for 15 Jan, and the contractor has not acknowledged the booking. The diagnostic may find work that exceeds the remaining £82. The interim welfare needs are unresolved and a property manager may need to decide them.
