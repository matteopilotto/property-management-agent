# Case 005: reasoning

## Facts

- Diagnostic visit approved up to £95 + VAT (events 7, 8).
- Invoice NBL-005-INV-71 is £95 + £19 VAT = £114, with remittance 00-00-00 / 00000000 (invoice-NBL-005-INV-71.pdf, event 18).
- Event 19 asks for payment to 11-11-11 / 11111111 with no independent verification.
- The £460 + VAT repair was not approved; the Landlord wants a breakdown and attendance record first (events 15, 16).
- The job sheet was requested three times and not supplied (events 12, 14, 17).
- No payment instruction entered (event 20).

## Conflicts

- Two different sets of remittance details (invoice file vs event 19).
- Event 11 reports a failed control board, while event 18 says the fault did not recur during the visit.

## Missing

- Independent verification of the bank detail change via a phone number already on file.
- Written job sheet and itemised estimate.
- Landlord decision on the £460 + VAT repair.

## Options rejected

- Approve or forward the invoice for payment: the bank details are unverified.
- Email the contractor to confirm the change: an email reply from the same channel cannot verify it, so a call-back on file contact details is needed.
- Wait: this is a payment-security decision and the invoice is due 19 May.

## Uncertainty

We cannot tell whether the bank change is genuine or fraudulent. The diagnostic amount itself looks correct.
