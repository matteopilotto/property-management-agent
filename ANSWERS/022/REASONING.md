# Case 022: reasoning

## Facts

- The landlord complained that the response took too long (event 29).
- The tenant said on 24 Sept that no replacement had arrived and there was no packaging (event 30).
- On 25 Sept the tenant said 'the job is completed' but also asked whether a planned washing-machine change was still happening (event 31).
- The landlord said a new machine was ordered and delivery, setup and disposal were arranged, with no date given (events 27, 29).
- The landlord asked for a quote on 21 Sept (event 28) but said the same day they had sorted it themselves (event 29).
- The 9 Sept engineer visit has no confirmed outcome (events 18, 21, 22).

## Conflicts

- Event 31 says the job is completed, but event 30 says nothing was delivered and event 31 asks if the change is still happening.
- Event 28 asks the agency for a quote, but event 29 says the landlord had already arranged the replacement.
- Event 23 gives the unit height in cm and event 24 gives it in mm.
- There is no evidence the engineer attended, and the replacement plan appeared without any recorded repair outcome.

## Missing

- Delivery and installation date and installer
- Confirmation of whether the washer-dryer is now working or replaced
- Engineer visit outcome
- Appliance identification details from the tenant

## Options rejected

- Ask the tenant to confirm completion: the landlord has complained about delay, and the landlord's and tenant's accounts conflict, so one message would not settle it.
- Close the case: there is no evidence of completion, and the tenant's own message contradicts it.
- Wait: the landlord has complained about delay, and the playbook says to escalate.

## Uncertainty

The tenant's 'job completed' reply (event 31) may refer to a different follow-up, so I can't tell whether the machine has been replaced.
