# Case 028: reasoning

## Facts

- Pineglass quoted £564.48 for the bundled works (event 11, provider-quotation.pdf).
- The landlord declined the quote and said she would arrange her own company (event 17).
- The agency told the landlord it would wait for her to confirm completion (event 19) and told Pineglass not to attend (event 20).
- The landlord wrote that the work had been completed (event 21).
- The request was marked completed (event 22), and the pending workflow task was dismissed (event 24).
- The property is vacant, and the agency stood the contractor down before any visit, so no keys were issued (events 3, 20).

## Conflicts

- Event 1 says landlord approval was marked as already given for the move-out works. Events 12 to 17 show the agency asked the landlord for approval and she then declined the quote. The later events are treated as authoritative.
- The landlord's completion message gives no date, photos or invoice (event 21).

## Missing

- Completion evidence from the landlord's own contractor: photos, invoice and date.
- Reply to the request for a cost breakdown and any former-tenant responsibility (event 23).

## Options rejected

- Instruct Pineglass: the landlord declined its quote.
- Charge the tenant: there is no evidence of costs or tenant responsibility.
- Escalate: the record does not contradict itself in a way that decides the move.
- Wait for the breakdown reply: the landlord has confirmed completion and the playbook closes without payment in this case.

## Uncertainty

Completion rests only on the landlord's unsupported email. The breakdown request in event 23 is unanswered, and a reply could reopen the tenancy account question.
