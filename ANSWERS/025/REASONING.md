# Case 025: reasoning

## Facts

- The landlord approved the plumbing work generally, with no amount stated (events 2-3).
- The contractor never gave a quote despite requests (events 4, 5, 8, 11).
- The visit was confirmed for 10 September (event 16), but no completion message or visit report is recorded.
- The invoice for a new mono kitchen tap was corrected to the right postcode (events 19-22, corrected-plumbing-invoice.pdf). It has a reference and a description but no amount, VAT or date of work.
- The tenant's loose-drawer report (event 12) is a separate issue and is unanswered.
- The boiler remark in the call (event 21) is not part of this job.

## Conflicts

- The tenant asked about a possible new tap and limescale, but the contractor replaced the tap with no recorded diagnosis or quote.
- The invoice postcode was wrong at first and has been corrected.
- Event 13 notes confusion over the property address.

## Missing

- Invoice amount and VAT
- Date of attendance and confirmation the work is complete
- Warranty on the new tap
- Tenant confirmation that the drip has stopped and whether the water is still cloudy
- Landlord approval of any cost once the amount is known

## Options rejected

- Forward the invoice to the landlord for payment: there is no amount to forward and no quote to compare it with.
- Approve the invoice: it cannot be checked against any quote or approved sum.
- Close the case: completion is unconfirmed, and the drawer and cloudy-water issues are open.
- Escalate: one message to the contractor can settle what is missing.

## Uncertainty

It is not certain that the tap was fitted on 10 September, because no visit report exists. The cost is unknown, so it may exceed what the landlord expected and need fresh approval.
