# Case 010: reasoning

## Facts

- Landlord approval recorded only for the original laminate-repair scope (event 6).
- Landlord asked for estimates for a full kitchen and for cupboards and doors only, which is not approval (event 16).
- Contractor's full-kitchen figures: £4,850 + VAT supply, £3,800 + VAT fitting, £230 + VAT waste, £380 + VAT electrical estimate (event 24).
- Figures forwarded to the landlord and his reply is awaited (events 25, 26).
- Commission basis, cap and VAT treatment unresolved (events 22, 23).
- No visit booked and the tenant's first offered date has passed (events 10, 21).

## Conflicts

- Contractor says a full kitchen is the practical option (events 12, 14), but there is no site visit or written assessment, and the approved scope is a laminate repair.
- The contractor's earlier full-kitchen quote was never supplied (event 12).
- The commission and VAT basis was left unsettled (events 22, 23), so the figures sent to the landlord (event 25) may change.
- The cupboards-and-doors-only estimate is missing (event 24).

## Missing

- Settled commission basis, cap, VAT treatment and total payable
- Cupboards-and-doors-only estimate
- Landlord decision or approval
- Booked site visit and dog-access arrangements

## Options rejected

- Wait for the landlord's reply: the quote the landlord is being asked to approve has unresolved money terms that only the property manager can settle.
- Instruct the contractor: there is no landlord approval for replacement work.
- Ask the landlord: no single message would settle the commission, cap and VAT question.

## Uncertainty

The landlord may reply before the property manager acts. It is unclear whether the commission affects the landlord's price or only the agency's terms with the contractor.
