# Case 006: reasoning

## Facts

- Event 1: landlord requested current solar generation readings; no fault or contractor visit requested.
- Event 7: moved to manual handling as an information request; no contractor selected.
- Events 6, 10, 13, 16: tenant asked repeatedly for reading or photo.
- Event 17: tenant says readings were supplied about two weeks earlier by his wife's call and email, but gave no figures or image.

## Conflicts

- Tenant says readings were already supplied (event 17); no such reply or figure appears anywhere in the request's correspondence (events 9, 12, 15).

## Missing

- The actual kWh reading or meter/inverter photo
- Date and channel of the tenant's earlier supply

## Options rejected

- Escalate: one message to the tenant can settle it and no liability, money or safety issue is involved.
- Wait: the tenant just replied and a reply is needed.
- Instruct a contractor: no repair is requested and approval is not for works.
- Close: no reading has been delivered to the landlord.

## Uncertainty

The earlier readings may exist in another channel (the wife's phone call or email to the office) that is not linked to this request. If the tenant gives no figure, someone should check the office inbox and phone log.
