# Case 020: reasoning

## Facts

- Tenant reports a hot smell and a warm socket near the kitchen counter, still noticeable at 16:42 (events 1, 17, 25).
- The handyman does not do fixed wiring and has no test equipment (events 11, 15).
- The landlord has not approved an electrician or electrical repairs (events 6, 16).
- The handyman did not enter because nobody answered and he had no clear key authority (events 19, 23).
- The landlord has not replied to the call or the email asking for an immediate decision (events 20, 24).
- The tenant had no emergency guidance until now (events 9, 25). A 10-year-old child is at the property (event 4).

## Conflicts

- The landlord's text says 'have a look if you can get in' (event 21) but gives no key authority and no electrician approval (events 16, 20).
- The handyman is not qualified for the electrical assessment the situation needs (events 11, 15).

## Missing

- Landlord authority for a qualified electrician and for access.
- Any electrical test, photo or temperature reading.
- Confirmation of circuit isolation.

## Options rejected

- Ask the landlord again: already chased three times with no reply, and the safety risk needs a decision by the property manager.
- Instruct a contractor: there is no landlord approval for an electrician.
- Wait: the hazard is live and a delay is unsafe.
- Rely on the handyman: not competent, and no access authority.

## Uncertainty

The real severity of the fault is unknown because nothing has been tested. The landlord's current whereabouts and intentions are unknown.
