# Case 007: reasoning

- Landlord approval (event 3) was in reply to a TV-only request (event 2); no spending limit was recorded (event 4).
- The downstairs light was never put to the landlord (events 2, 5).
- The landlord said they do not remember leaving a TV (event 3). The tenant thinks the board is fried and is open to removing the TV (event 15).
- The landlord's auto-reply says they are away (event 7).
- The tenant offered slots (event 16). Bracken Spark proposed 29 Sept after 15:00, with no exact time and no confirmation sent (event 19).
- The rear-door handle is a separate matter and has not been reported (events 15, 18).

## Conflicts

- The landlord's approval covers only the TV, but the visit request combines the TV and the light (events 3, 17).
- TV ownership and liability are unresolved (event 3).

## Missing

- Landlord approval for the light work
- Whether the TV is the landlord's, and whether to assess it
- A spending limit
- An exact arrival time from the electrician

## Options rejected

- Confirming the 29 Sept visit now: the light work has no landlord approval and the TV scope is uncertain.
- Escalating: one landlord message can settle both questions.
- Charging the tenant: there is no evidence of tenant damage or of who owns the TV.

## Uncertainty

The landlord is away, so a reply may be slow. If there is no reply by the follow-up date, chase again and consider escalating if the silence continues.
