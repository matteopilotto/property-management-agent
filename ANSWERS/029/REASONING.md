# Case 029: reasoning

## Facts

- No work order or appointment on record (events 2, 13)
- Landlord asked someone to look at boiler pressure loss but fixed no date or access (event 5)
- Landlord authorised a diagnostic visit only by a suitably identified contractor, and has not agreed a key drop or entry while the tenant is out (event 14)
- Contractor gave no work reference, business address or company contact (events 9, 12, 16)
- Tenant is free Saturday 17 May 10:00-12:00 if visitor and purpose are confirmed, and does not want her son used as an access contact (events 6, 15)
- Contractor proposed 08:00-13:00 and a key in the shared meter cabinet (events 18, 20)
- No tenant consent to share her number (event 17)
- Landlord has not replied to the 14 May clarification request (event 19)

## Conflicts

- The contractor says the landlord asked him to check the boiler (event 9). The landlord cannot confirm his identity or current engagement (events 5, 14).
- The contractor's 08:00-13:00 Saturday window does not match the tenant's 10:00-12:00 availability (events 15, 20).

## Missing

- Landlord confirmation that Bram Alder / Hearthline is the intended contractor
- Contractor's verified business and insurance details
- A confirmed appointment window agreed with the tenant
- Tenant consent before sharing her phone number

## Options rejected

- Instructing or booking the contractor: identity unverified and access not agreed
- Escalating now: the landlord's reply to a single outstanding question can settle the contractor identity
- Waiting without sending anything: the tenant needs a clear answer and the key-drop risk needs stopping

## Uncertainty

Whether the contractor is genuine is unknown. If the landlord and contractor do not answer, or the contractor keeps pushing the key drop, this should go to the property manager as a possible security risk.
