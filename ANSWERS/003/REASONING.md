# Case 003: reasoning

## Facts

- Landlord replied 'Yes, please proceed' (event 4).
- The visit took place on 12 Sept (events 17, 20).
- The invoice covers only the kitchen mixer tap and the basin hot tap insert and head (event 22, tap-works-invoice.pdf).
- The invoice shows no amount (tap-works-invoice.pdf).
- No findings on the vent leak or bedroom damp are recorded (events 21, 22).
- The landlord asked for an insurance check and a review of the roof-leak history, and neither is recorded as done (events 4, 5, 12).

## Conflicts

- The invoice names a different property address from the canonical one. I treated this as noise and did not repeat it (tap-works-invoice.pdf).
- Event 14 said the job was done on 11 Sept, but event 15 corrected this to 'Booked for tomorrow' and the visit was on 12 Sept (event 20). This is a crossed message, not a contradiction.

## Missing

- Invoice amount and VAT treatment.
- Invoice reissued with the correct property address.
- Contractor's findings and any quote for the vent leak and bedroom damp.
- Confirmation that both taps are fixed.
- Insurance and roof-leak history review requested by the landlord.

## Options rejected

- Forward the invoice to the landlord for payment: there is no amount and the property is wrong.
- Close the case: the vent leak and damp are not addressed and the landlord's requests are open.
- Escalate now: one message to the contractor can settle the missing details.
- Wait without messaging: the contractor needs to be prompted.

## Uncertainty

The roof-leak, insurance and liability questions may need the property manager once the contractor reports. The landlord's insurance and history requests still need action.
