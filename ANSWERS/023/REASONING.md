# Case 023: reasoning

## Facts

- Landlord authorised diagnosis only, up to £180 including VAT (event 5).
- Diagnosis quote was £138 including VAT (event 7).
- The £372 estimate was sent after the 5 Feb visit and asked for written authorisation (event 9).
- Landlord had not decided on the estimate and authorised no further spend (event 12).
- The contractor was still checking parts and estimate details on 7 Feb (event 16).
- Invoices A (£138) and B (£372) are both dated 10 Feb with a service date of 5 Feb (events 17-18; invoice PDFs).
- No signed completion record, no payment and no written authorisation for actuator work is on file (event 19).
- The tenant does not know whether the actuator was replaced, and heating is still intermittent (event 20).
- The contractor could not clarify or give evidence when asked on 11 Feb (events 21-22).
- The landlord was asked how to proceed on 12 Feb and has not replied (event 23).

## Conflicts

- Invoice B says actuator replacement was completed on 5 Feb, but the estimate was sent later that day, parts were unconfirmed on 7 Feb, and the tenant is unaware of any replacement.
- Invoices A and B overlap on attendance and diagnosis, and neither says whether the other is credited or superseded.
- Invoice B (£372) is above the £180 routine limit and has no landlord approval.

## Missing

- Contractor job notes, parts record and signed completion evidence
- Landlord's decision on the invoices and on any further work
- Clarification of which invoice is current and whether the diagnosis charge is credited

## Options rejected

- Approve or forward invoice A or B: unauthorised, contradictory and overlapping.
- Ask the contractor again: it has been asked twice and could not answer.
- Wait on the landlord only: the money dispute and the safety angle need property manager judgement.
- Instruct the actuator replacement: the landlord has not authorised it.

## Uncertainty

The landlord may reply to event 23 and settle the invoice question. It is unknown whether the actuator was replaced.
