# Case 017: reasoning

## Facts

- Door catches at the top and the latch needs a firm pull (events 1, 3, 15).
- The building file lists flat entrance doors as fire-resisting but holds no inspection record (events 5, 13).
- A previous common-area check noted this door closing slowly, with no measurement (event 13).
- The contractor's visit was visual only (event 10).
- Quote NBL-017-Q1 is £186.00, valid to 28 Feb 2025, and excludes door adjustment, hardware work and any fire-door inspection (fire-door-quotation.pdf, events 11, 16).
- The landlord asked whether the quote can be approved from the repair budget but wants the scope and building response confirmed before booking (event 17).
- The building manager's response on scope is pending (events 18, 19).
- No work has been booked (event 19).

## Conflicts

- The landlord asked whether the issue could be limited to decorating (event 7), but the evidence points to a closing or fit fault that painting would not address and could mask (events 10, 13).
- The landlord's reply (event 17) is conditional and is not a clear approval to book.

## Missing

- Building manager's reply on whether the painting needs building approval (event 18).
- Any fire-door inspection or assessment of the door.
- The landlord's answer on additional inspection costs (event 14).
- A clear landlord approval of the quote after the scope is settled.

## Options rejected

- Book Pale Finch Decorating for the £186.00 painting: the landlord's approval is conditional, the building response is pending, and painting could mask a possible fire-door fault.
- Wait only for the building manager: safety is involved and the scope and liability decisions sit with the property manager.
- Ask the landlord to approve the quote: the quote does not address the closing fault, so approving it alone would not settle the case.

## Uncertainty

The door's actual fire rating and the cause of the contact at the top edge are unknown, as is the building manager's view. Painting alone may be acceptable if the building manager replies that it is.
