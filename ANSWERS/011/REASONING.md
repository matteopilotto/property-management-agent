# Case 011: reasoning

## Facts

- Tenant reported a damp patch and drip in the kitchen ceiling (event 1).
- The contractor saw a leaking joint he could not reach through the hatch (events 9, 11).
- The Landlord authorised the quoted plumbing scope only, up to the estimate (event 13).
- On 13 June the contractor found an unidentified pale, fibrous-looking lining and paused. He was told not to disturb it (events 17, 18).
- The contractor left without opening the ceiling and cannot price work involving the lining (event 23).
- The leak recurred when the tap was used. The tenant has stopped using that tap (event 20).
- The Landlord said her approval excludes any material assessment and asked for cost and timing first (event 22).
- The Landlord was already asked for a decision on further scope (event 24).

## Conflicts

- No factual contradictions. The approved scope cannot be carried out as quoted because of the unidentified lining.

## Missing

- Identification of the lining material
- Cost and timing of any specialist assessment
- Landlord authorisation for any additional scope
- The quote amount is not stated in the case events

## Options rejected

- Instruct the contractor to proceed: would require drilling or cutting near an unidentified material, and the Landlord has not approved it.
- Ask the Landlord again: the Landlord was already asked in event 24 and has no records. The hazard needs a property manager decision.
- Wait: the leak is active and a possible safety risk remains.
- Close: the repair is not done.

## Uncertainty

The lining has not been identified, so whether it is hazardous is unknown. The quote amount is not visible in the case.
