- Water drips from the Flat 2 ceiling light fitting and the bulge is enlarging (events 2, 12, 17, 23).
- Plumber and building manager found no source or isolation point (16, 21).

- Flat 3 tenant refuses entry until she is home after 17:30 (9, 20).
- Landlord has not authorised forced or unaccompanied entry (6, 11, 19).
- No documented staff authority to override the tenant's refusal; full tenancy wording unchecked (22).

## Conflicts

- No contradiction in the record. Flat 3 is only a suspected source (8) and the tenancy access and emergency wording is unclear (19).

## Missing

- Full tenancy agreement wording
- Agency director decision on entry
- Electrician's findings
- Confirmed leak source and isolation point

## Options rejected

- Instruct a contractor to enter Flat 3: no consent or authority.
- Wait: the leak is active and there is a safety risk.
- Ask one party: no single message settles the entry authority; it needs a director decision.

## Uncertainty

The source of the leak is unknown, and whether Flat 3 is the source is unconfirmed. The outcome of the electrician's visit is unknown.
