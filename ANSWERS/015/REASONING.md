# Case 015: reasoning

## Facts

- Property is vacant; keys are at the office (events 1, 2)
- Brindlewick quoted £665 on 8 Sep (event 6, property-preparation-quotation.pdf)
- The landlord replied she was happy to approve if the cost is recovered from the bond, and repeated this (events 8, 10)
- The deposit held is £650, and costs plus damage exceed it; a deposit claim is being prepared (event 11)
- The contractor was asked on 10 Sep not to book, and the hold was confirmed on 16 Sep (events 12, 15)
- The landlord sent 'approved quote' on 17 Sep with no quote identified (event 16)
- The property manager and coordinator both record that the sanitation work waits for the separate maintenance to be done first (events 18, 19, 20, 21)
- No maintenance completion date is recorded and no visit is booked (events 18, 21)

## Conflicts

- The landlord's approval is conditional on bond recovery, but the contractor was told simply that the quote was approved (events 8, 9, 10).
- The landlord's 17 Sep 'approved quote' doesn't say which quote it means. It may refer to the separate maintenance quote (event 16, event 15).
- The £665 quote includes carpet shampooing, which is not in the inspection items. It does not expressly cover making good the disturbed soil (events 1, 6, inspection report).
- The landlord was asked to approve the quote only in general terms, without the carpet and garden tasks listed (event 7).
- The first report sent to the contractor was the wrong one (events 4, 5).

## Missing

- Completion or scheduling of the separate major maintenance
- Clear landlord approval of the £665 sanitation quote, including whether the bond condition still applies
- A confirmed contractor slot
- Final itemised figures for the deposit claim

## Options rejected

- Instruct or book the contractor now: the hold is still in force, the maintenance isn't done, and the approval is conditional and unclear.
- Escalate now: the property manager has set a short wait, and nothing needs deciding until the maintenance is done or the wait expires.
- Ask the landlord now: the property manager has told us to wait three working days. The approval check is noted for the follow-up.
- Close: the work has not been done.

## Uncertainty

If the maintenance is still not scheduled on 30 Sep, the case may need chasing or escalating. The bond condition and the unclear approval will need settling before booking.
