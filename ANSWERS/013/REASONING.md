# Case 013: reasoning

## Facts

- Landlord was told £150 + VAT, £180 total (event 9). He replied he was happy to use the contractor if £180 was the total (event 10). The quote was recorded as approved (event 12).
- On 2 Sep the contractor said the glass had still not arrived and no appointment was arranged (event 21).
- Event 22 (9 Sep) records no appointment confirmation or visit report.
- Invoice NBL-013-INV-09 dated 10 Sep says 'Supply and fit 1 pane of glass' but the amount is blank (event 23, glazing-invoice.pdf).

## Conflicts

- An invoice for supply and fit exists, but no visit or completion is recorded and the glass was still undelivered on 2 Sep (events 21-23).
- The approval note records the quote as approved (event 12). The landlord's reply was conditional on £180 being the total (event 10). These are consistent if £150 + VAT = £180, which the contractor has not confirmed.

## Missing

- Confirmation the glass was fitted, and the date
- Invoice amount, with a corrected copy
- Completion photos
- Whether the tenant's damp and cold concern was resolved. No assessment is recorded (event 5).

## Options rejected

- Forward the invoice to the landlord for payment: there is no amount and no proof of completion.
- Approve the invoice: no amount to check against the quote.
- Close the case: there is no completion evidence and the final checks have not been done.
- Escalate: one message to the contractor can settle it, and the contractor is responsive.

## Uncertainty

The work may have been done between 2 and 10 Sep without being recorded. The contractor has not replied to the request yet.
