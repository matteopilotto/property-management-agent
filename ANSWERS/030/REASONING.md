# Case 030: reasoning

## Facts

- Tenant removed the beeping alarm, leaving a ceiling opening (events 1, 2); the fault was marked urgent (event 2).
- Landlord said he would do the work himself (events 7, 10); no contractor or date was given (event 11).
- Landlord claimed completion on 31 Aug and mentioned the ten-year life of the alarm (event 12).
- Landlord's socket details cover only a back-bedroom and a kitchen socket (event 14), while the tenant reported several sockets (event 5).
- The tenant has not confirmed completion (events 9-15).

## Conflicts

- Landlord's completion claim (event 12) does not say the alarm was replaced or the ceiling opening closed.
- The tenant reported several loose sockets (event 5), but the landlord's account covers only two (event 14).
- Event 14 dates the kitchen socket fix to 5 Aug, before the tenant's 20 Aug report; the landlord ties the loose socket to boiler work in Feb 2025.

## Missing

- Tenant confirmation that a working alarm is fitted and the ceiling opening is closed
- Landlord confirmation of who did the work and what was fitted
- Status of the remaining sockets

## Options rejected

- Close the case: the completion claim is unverified and the item is a safety one.
- Escalate: one message to the tenant can settle the point.
- Instruct a contractor: the landlord is handling the work himself.

## Uncertainty

If the tenant says no alarm is fitted, or does not reply, this should go to the property manager because it is a safety issue.
