# Case 002: reasoning

## Facts

- Tenancy file has only a 16 Oct 2024 gas record (event 2).
- Landlord instructed the annual check only and will not approve extra spend without asking her (events 5, 16, 23).
- Visit on 9 Oct was recorded satisfactory, with boiler and cooker on the job card (events 9, 19, gas-portal-record.pdf).
- Flintmere has paused the signed paperwork over an installation-detail query and gave no date (events 19, 21, 24).
- The portal record shows 'Customer document: Processing' (gas-portal-record.pdf).
- Tenant reports lockouts on events 3, 17 and 22, with no gas smell, and offers access after 21 Oct (event 22).
- A lockout diagnostic is a separate job and has not been authorised (events 19, 23).

## Conflicts

- The portal and engineer say 'satisfactory', but no signed certificate exists and Flintmere is querying an installation detail.
- The landlord offered the portal screenshot as the paperwork (event 12), but it is not the signed document.
- A satisfactory gas check sits alongside repeated unexplained boiler lockouts.

## Missing

- Signed gas safety record from Flintmere and a date for it
- Detail of the installation query
- Landlord decision on a lockout diagnostic
- Whether the 2024 certificate is still in date

## Options rejected

- Ask the landlord again: she has already been asked twice (events 4, 20) and has declined to authorise a diagnostic (event 23).
- Book a diagnostic: the landlord has not approved spend.
- Wait: the file review is due, the tenant keeps reporting lockouts on a gas appliance, and the compliance paperwork is stalled with no date.
- Send the portal screenshot to the tenant as the certificate: it is only a 'Processing' entry.

## Uncertainty

The case does not say whether the 2024 certificate has expired or what the installation-detail query is. The lockouts may be harmless, but nobody has diagnosed them.
