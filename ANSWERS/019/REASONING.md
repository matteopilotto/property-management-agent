# Case 019: reasoning

## Facts

- Landlord approved proceeding with remedial works, no price limit (event 5)
- Provider asked for quote only; no work until written authority (events 7, 8)
- Tenant vacated 27 Aug and returned keys; agency held a set (events 3, 7)
- No provider reply to chases (events 10, 11); on 10 Sept call Rowan said he had completed the work the previous day (event 13)
- Invoice £174.00, single lump sum, no itemisation (remedial-works-invoice.pdf, events 15, 16)
- No appointment, access, visit narrative or completion evidence on file (event 15)

## Conflicts

- Contractor says he thought he was instructed to do the work, but the emails asked for a quote only (events 8, 13)
- Note in event 12 refers to awaiting an invoice before any invoice existed
- Invoice scope (replaced floorboard, new Venetian blind) differs from the request (cracked chipboard, blind needing repair)
- Contractor reports work done despite no access arrangement on file and the tenant having left

## Missing

- Access date and method
- Before/after photos
- Itemised pricing and VAT status
- Explanation of scope
- Landlord acceptance of the £174 cost

## Options rejected

- Forwarding the invoice to the landlord for payment: work is unverified and was done without authority or a quote
- Escalating immediately: one message to the contractor can settle access and completion; escalate if the reply is missing or unsatisfactory
- Approving the invoice: no approved quote exists

## Uncertainty

The work may not have been done, or may have been done without proper access. The landlord's general approval may cover £174, but the landlord has not yet seen the price.
